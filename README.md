## Key EDA Findings

The exploratory analysis identified several notable patterns:

 > Likes and retweets showed an extremely strong positive correlation (r = 0.99847).
 > Average engagement varied considerably across sentiment categories.
 > Motivation and Mesmerizing were among the sentiment categories with the highest average engagement.
 > Engagement varied by posting hour, with late-evening posts showing relatively high average likes in this dataset.
 > Rare sentiment categories require caution when interpreting their average engagement.
